# Vehicle registration renewal — demo policy

## Insurance requirement
A vehicle should have valid motor insurance for the registration renewal period. If insurance is close to expiry, confirm continuous coverage with the insurer before submitting a renewal request. The assistant must not assume the policy has been renewed.

## Outstanding fines
Outstanding traffic fines may need to be settled before vehicle registration renewal. Review the official traffic-fines channel and confirm the current amount before payment. This demo never collects payment.

## Required information
A renewal request commonly requires a valid identity record, vehicle registration details and valid insurance information. The demo checks only synthetic profile fields; it does not verify identity or official eligibility.

## Human confirmation
Before a renewal action is submitted, the citizen must review the vehicle identifier and proposed action and explicitly approve it. The demo approval step only authorizes a simulated transaction.

## Service disclaimer
This is synthetic training content, not official UAE government policy. Verify requirements with the relevant official authority.
